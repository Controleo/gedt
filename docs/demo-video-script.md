# Demonstration video script (about 7 minutes)

Record the screen and your voice with any free recorder (for example OBS, or Zoom recording to yourself). Use the hosted address, sign in fresh, and reset the demo data first. Speak slowly. Keep the video under 8 minutes and add it to the questionnaire page so evaluators can watch it before they try the system.

| Time | On screen | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Title slide or sign-in page | "This is a prototype of a shared map of telecom devices and fibre routes for several operators. The data is invented. It is built to test an idea, not to run a real network." |
| 0:30 | Sign-in page, click Asset Manager | "There are four roles. I will start as an Asset Manager at a fictional operator, Lagoon Telecom." |
| 0:50 | Map, point at legend | "Colour shows status and shape shows device type. Solid lines are this operator's routes. The grey dotted lines are routes other operators chose to share." |
| 1:30 | Click a device, then Update status | "Clicking an item shows its details and actions. Every status change is recorded with who made it." |
| 2:15 | + Route, draw 3 points, Finish, fill the form, Register | "Routes are drawn as lines. I give it a code, fibre counts and choose Shared, which lets other operators see a limited view." |
| 3:15 | Search LAG-EVAL-01, open it, Report cut | "To report a cut, I find the route and press Report cut. The system marks it, and warns the owner, operators connected to it, and operators with routes within 100 metres." |
| 4:00 | Alerts page | "Here is the alert. I can acknowledge it, and resolve it when the repair is done." |
| 4:30 | Sign out, sign in as the other operator, Alerts | "Now I am a different operator. I only get a general warning. The route and owner are not shown, because that is not mine to know." |
| 5:15 | Free routes page | "This lists routes that operators have shared, that are working and have spare fibres." |
| 5:45 | Dashboard, Reports and export | "Managers get an overview, maintenance reports and exports in GeoJSON and CSV." |
| 6:30 | Back to a slide | "Please try the five tasks on the task sheet, then answer the questionnaire. It takes about 20 minutes. Thank you." |
